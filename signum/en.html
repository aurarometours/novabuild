07 FAQ

Questions before we start.

How long does it take?+

A Signal website takes 2–3 weeks on average from preview to launch. Software and apps follow a dedicated roadmap we define together.

I have no copy or photos. Is that a problem?+

No. We write the copy and guide you on photos and video, or we use professional material that fits your brand.

How does the free preview work?+

We show you a first visual direction before any payment. If it convinces you, you pay 50% and we start; the balance is due on delivery.

Who owns the domain and website?+

You do. Domain, content and website are registered to you, with no lock-in.

What happens after launch?+

You get 30 days of revisions included. After that you can choose the Care plan or ask us for changes when you need them.

Why not a cheap template?+

Because a template looks like what it is. A custom website is faster, stands out from competitors and is built to get you contacted.

Do you work outside Italy?+

Yes. We are based in Rome and work remotely with clients in Europe, the UK, the US and the UAE, in English and Italian.
